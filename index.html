What the game accesses and why

With your permission, Duck Collector reads aggregated step totals from Android Health Connect to show daily steps, award eggs, advance incubation, and track lifetime milestones. It also reads the identities of the apps supplying those totals to display the step source. The app can recheck recent calendar-day totals since enrollment within a window of up to 28 prior days plus today. Today's steps may count when you first connect. It does not request GPS location, heart rate, contacts, camera, microphone, or permission to write health records. A compatible device or fitness app must supply the steps to Health Connect.

Optional background access

If you enable hatch alerts and grant background step access on a supported device, Android periodically runs step checks while Duck Collector is not open to update progress and support local hatch notifications. Android can delay those checks. Foreground step play does not require background access or notifications. Demo mode reads no health data and has separate progress.

Daily feather reminders

If enabled, Duck Collector checks locally whether you have visited today and whether optional ad rewards remain. It may show one evening reminder in your phone’s local time. The visit timestamp, reminder preference and last reminder timestamp stay on your device, are not sent to advertisers, and are not included in collection exports. This reminder does not require background Health Connect access. Turn it off in Settings or Android notification settings. Watching rewarded ads remains optional.

Information stored on your device

The game stores step totals, recent daily credit totals, enrollment and sync timestamps, connection timezone, eggs, collected ducks, feather balance, claimed lifetime milestones, selected pond residents, decoration inventory and placement, pond activity/friendship state, notification and animation preferences, and other game settings. If you restore a backup, an undo copy of previous progress is kept locally. Some legacy pond activity fields remain in saves for compatibility even where their old controls are no longer displayed.

We also store the accepted terms version, operator identifier, and acceptance timestamp on this device. The acceptance confirms that the age and terms checkboxes were selected; it does not record a date of birth or verify a person's identity. The acknowledgement is not included in exported game backups and is not sent to the developer.

Security and automatic backup

Game-save payloads, including local restore copies, use AES-GCM encryption with a key managed by Android Keystore and Android private app storage. The local terms acknowledgement uses private app preferences. Automatic Android app backup and device transfer are disabled. These protections do not guarantee absolute security; keep your device secure and updated.

What the developer receives and shares

This version has no accounts, developer game server, billing, or automatic cloud sync. It includes Google AdMob banners on Today, optional rewarded ads, and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) for ad privacy choices. Internet access supports those services. The developer can receive aggregate ad-performance and revenue reports through AdMob; no separate gameplay analytics service is installed. Duck Collector does not automatically transmit your steps, collection, or terms acknowledgement to the developer. It does not sell health information or share it for advertising, lending, insurance eligibility, or data-broker purposes. Artwork is bundled with the app; gameplay sends no player data to an AI service.

Today banner and optional rewarded ads

Starting in version 1.4.4, the regular Today screen automatically requests a small Google AdMob banner above the step counter when applicable ad privacy choices allow a request. The banner may refresh while Today is visible. The app has no switch to hide it. You can continue playing when there is no ad. The banner gives no reward, does not cover the game controls, and is absent from demo mode and other screens. In version 1.4.3, the banner was off until enabled and could be turned off in Settings.

In the Feather Shop, you may choose to watch a rewarded ad for one feather, up to two completed rewards per day. Rewarded ads are requested only after you continue from the ad explanation. You can walk, hatch, and collect ducks without watching a rewarded ad.

Production builds check ad privacy choices using Google's User Messaging Platform before requesting a banner. Where required, Google presents privacy choices and you can revisit them in Settings → Ads & privacy. Accepting the game terms is separate from ad consent. The 1.4.4 phone preview automatically requests Google's sample banner on Today and shows a sample label if it cannot load. Preview ads do not generate revenue.

Google's ad and consent services may process your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device or other identifiers, app/ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and manage privacy choices. Google reports that its mobile ads SDK encrypts collected data in transit. The app removes the Android advertising-ID permission; this does not prevent processing of other identifiers or network data. Ads are requested without personalization and with a general-audience content filter. Non-personalized ads can still process data and may require consent. We never provide Health Connect records, step totals, step sources, walking milestones, or your duck/pond collection to the ad system, and we do not use them for ad selection or targeting.

When you review the 1.4.4 update, you select or confirm 13–17 or 18 or older. This age band is stored locally without a birth date. A restrictive under-age-of-consent flag is sent to Google for the 13–17 choice. You can correct the choice in Settings. Google processes ad/consent data under its policies, including https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Availability, privacy options, retention and rights depend on your region and Google's practices; you can use those resources for requests concerning data held by Google.

Local ad records

We store the daily reward count, a timezone fixed when rewards are first checked, and up to 64 recent reward identifiers locally to prevent duplicate credits. A pending receipt is kept until its reward is handled. The age band and SDK consent preferences are stored in private app storage. The reward ledger is encrypted with the game-save key; the age choice and pending receipt use private preferences. None of these settings or receipts is included in portable game backups. Import and undo do not reset the daily reward count. Clearing app data removes these local records and settings, but does not itself erase data already held by Google.

Limited edition walking events

When an event is active, the app uses permitted Health Connect step totals within that event's time window to track its walking goal. Event progress and earned ducks are saved locally with your collection and included in manual backups. They are not sent to the developer or shared with the ad system. Event dates use the phone's clock; there is no server account or online event verification.

Manual backups and outside services

You may export a portable JSON backup using Android's file picker. It contains your saved collection, pond state, milestones, step totals, recent daily credit totals, and timestamps. Exported files are NOT encrypted. A checksum detects some corruption and is not a password, encryption, or guarantee against tampering. If you choose a cloud or third-party storage provider, that provider receives the file at your direction under its own policies. Export only somewhere you trust and avoid public sharing.

Import replaces the current local progress after confirmation and normally keeps one on-device undo copy of the prior collection. A malformed prior save may instead be retained for recovery. Backups do not include or grant Android/Health Connect permissions or copy terms acceptance to another device. Google Play, Android, Health Connect, fitness-source apps, email services, and storage providers have their own practices. External links open a separate app and may expose normal connection information such as your IP address to the destination.

Retention and deletion

Recent daily credit totals are pruned during syncing after approximately 32 days. Lifetime progress, preferences, and the terms acknowledgement remain until deleted. Restore/recovery copies may remain until replaced or all local data is deleted. A manually exported backup can retain older records independently of the app's pruning.

Use Settings → Privacy, safety & support → Delete all progress & local data. You must type DELETE and confirm. This invokes Android's app-data reset, closes the app, and removes both real and demo progress, local game and step records, preferences, terms acknowledgement, local ad settings and reward records, and local restore/recovery copies. Android resets the app's runtime permissions. Review Health Connect access separately, especially on devices where Health Connect is a separate app. You can also use Android Settings → Apps → Duck Collector → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall. Menu labels vary by device.

Deletion has no in-app undo. It does NOT delete exported files, cloud-provider copies, email correspondence, records in Health Connect, or source fitness apps. Delete those separately with the relevant provider. A backup you keep can later restore its saved game information. There is no online Duck Collector account or developer-held game save to delete or recover.

Your choices

Skip rewarded ads at any time. Choosing not to watch does not reduce your normal game features or remove existing feathers. Ad privacy choices are separate from agreement to these terms and health permissions. Decline health access and use the separate demo or browse the collection interface. Revoke steps or background access in Health Connect at any time; real-step progression then stops updating normally. Disable hatch alerts in the app or Android notification settings. Revoking permissions does not itself delete saved progress. Terms acceptance is separate from health-data access. Declining terms exits gameplay without deleting an existing save; Android's app settings remain available for deletion.

Support correspondence

If you email twbmagic@gmail.com, the developer and email providers receive your address and what you send. We use correspondence to answer requests, investigate problems, and handle privacy or rights complaints. We do not sell it or use it for advertising. We retain it only as long as reasonably needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal or recordkeeping obligations, then delete it. Email-provider backup retention may follow its own policies. Request deletion by emailing us; we may ask for minimal information needed to verify the request and will explain any lawful reason that prevents complete deletion. Do not send health records or backup files unless necessary and specifically requested.

Age and audience

Duck Collector is intended for adults and teenagers age 13 and older, not children under 13. Users below their jurisdiction's age of legal adulthood must have a parent or guardian review and agree as required. The app asks for confirmation of age eligibility without collecting a birth date. It has no chat or user account service. If we learn that a child under 13 has sent personal information to support, we will delete it unless law requires retention, and ask a parent or guardian to handle further contact.

Changes and contact

For privacy questions, suspected security issues, or requests about support correspondence, contact twbmagic@gmail.com. Material policy changes will be disclosed appropriately before materially different data handling begins, and additional permission will be requested where required. The updated policy will appear in the app and on its public policy page. Do not rely on this policy as permission for undisclosed future advertising, tracking, or cloud services.

Optional reviews, sharing, and feedback

Rate app opens the Google Play listing. After you have used the game for a while, Google Play may also show its optional review card after a hatch. Reviews are handled by Google Play under Google's policies. Duck Collector cannot tell whether you submitted a rating and gives no reward for reviews. First-use and last-review-attempt dates, plus your exit-confirmation preference, stay in local app preferences. No steps, collection, or Health Connect data are attached to a review request.

Share app opens Android's share sheet with the public store link. Send feedback opens an email draft addressed to support with the app and Android versions; you decide what to add and whether to send it. Do not include health information or other sensitive details unless necessary for your request. Step-sync timings shown in Settings stay on this device; no performance analytics service was added.
